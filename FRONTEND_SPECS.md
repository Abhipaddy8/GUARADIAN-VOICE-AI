# FRONTEND_SPECS: Clinical & Family Interfaces

## 1. Admin Dashboard (React + Tailwind)
- **Fleet View:** Real-time grid of all active patients.
- **Crisis Monitor:** Pop-up window showing Gemini's "Internal Thought Process" and the live Webots stream.
- **Vitals Graph:** 60-second rolling window of HR and SpO2.

## 2. Family Portal
- **The "Anchor Vault":** Interface for families to upload voice samples and "Memory Anchors" (photos/stories).
- **Incident Reports:** Log of autonomous interventions and audio transcripts of AI-led de-escalations.
